A wrong departure time sends a person to a bus stop. A wrong amount on a purchase bill moves public money. This page points the same stack at a specimen purchase bill with planted defects: one model stage reads the cells with bounding boxes, geometry binds the rows, Google Search grounding checks the billed rates against live web prices, and deterministic checks — no model — do the accusing.

Honest limits
  • The bill is synthetic and clearly marked SPECIMEN; its three defects are planted so the checks have a measurable ground truth. Nothing here audits a real transaction.
  • Market ranges come from Google Search grounding at build time, and prices move; a production audit would re-ground per run and record its sources.
  • Findings are leads for a human auditor, not accusations. The system flags arithmetic, duplication and price outliers; intent is not machine-checkable.
  • A row with an illegible cell is withheld from checking, never guessed at — the same refusal rule the timetables use.
